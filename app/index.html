⚠ THE ONE MISSABLE TROPHY — DO THIS IN CHAPTER 1

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at all 18 spots) is the only trophy that can possibly be missed. One morning in Chapter 1, at Jason’s house, Lucia says she will meditate and walks to the balcony — follow her and accept the optional quest. That chance is gone after Chapter 1. Roadmap to the platinum — 3 steps

  1. STEP 1 · 60–80 HOURS

    Story + first habits

    Play all 5 chapters and take optional missions as they appear (100% needs any 100 of several hundred). Scan every new vehicle with WAiNK (100% needs 200 of 277 models) and study new animals with binoculars (100% needs 100 of 255 — no need to hunt). Turn on progress and criminal-profile notifications in the UI settings. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude. Covers: all 12 story trophies, the gold for the final mission, and early collectibles.

  2. STEP 2 · ABOUT 100 HOURS

    100% checklist + challenges

    After the story, the checklist opens under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion — earlier progress already counts, and nothing on it is missable. Lenient thresholds (about 60% true completion): 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 meditations, 300 stunts, all bank vaults, all drone races, all shooting ranges, all of Bret’s quests, 15 of 18 card sets. This step earns A Long Road (gold) and most silver and bronze trophies.

  3. STEP 3 · ABOUT 10 HOURS

    Strangers & Freaks + cleanup

    Strangers & Freaks is NOT part of 100%, so 8 stranger strands may still be waiting: finish them for the silver trophy. Then clean up the unconfirmed trophies (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) and any stragglers. The platinum Vice Got a Price! pops with the last trophy you earn.

Sources: roadmap steps and counts from PowerPyx and Sixshore — see each trophy’s cited source below.
